VIP Transportation

Aero Assist Global provides world-class ground transportation solutions for VIP passengers, flight crews, executives, and dignitaries across the Philippines. From luxury sedans and limousines to armored vehicles and helicopter transfers — our professional chauffeurs and transport coordinators ensure every journey is safe, comfortable, and on time.

Overview

Premium Ground Transportation across the Philippines

Every journey matters. Our VIP transportation service combines luxury vehicles, professional chauffeurs, and meticulous planning to deliver a seamless ground experience at every destination.

Our transportation desk coordinates all ground movement requirements — from single vehicle bookings to full motorcade arrangements for government and diplomatic missions.

  • Crew bus and van transfers for large groups
  • Helicopter and private transfer coordination
