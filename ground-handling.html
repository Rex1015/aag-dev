Overview

Premium Ground Handling for VIP Operations

Our ground handling team is trained to the highest international standards — delivering flawless service for VIP, VVIP, charter, and private aviation operations at every Philippine airport we serve.

What We Offer

From aircraft marshalling and ramp coordination to VIP passenger facilitation and cargo handling — our ground handling team manages every aspect of your turnaround with precision and care.

  • Aircraft marshalling and parking coordination
  • Passenger check-in and boarding assistance
  • Baggage handling and delivery
  • Cargo loading and unloading
  • Fueling coordination and supervision
  • Ground power unit (GPU) provision
  • Aircraft cleaning and cabin preparation
  • Catering uplift coordination
  • VIP lounge access and facilitation
  • Customs and immigration assistance
