Inflight Catering

Aero Assist Global provides premium inflight catering services for private jets, charter flights, VIP aircraft, and commercial airlines across the Philippines. From gourmet meals and fine dining experiences to special dietary menus and luxury beverage selections — our catering team ensures every meal served onboard reflects the highest standards of quality, presentation, and taste.

A simple and seamless process from your initial order to onboard delivery — ensuring your catering is always ready when your aircraft is.

01
📩

Submit Your Order

Send us your catering requirements including number of passengers, flight duration, dietary requirements, and any special requests or preferences.

02
🍽

Menu Customization

Our catering team prepares a customized menu proposal based on your requirements — including meal options, beverage selections, and special dietary needs.

03
✅

04

05

06

Onboard Excellence

Every meal is presented beautifully and served with the finest tableware and linens — creating a true fine dining experience at altitude.
