Overview

Professional CAAP Liaison Services

Our CAAP Liaison division specializes in managing all aviation regulatory requirements through direct coordination with the Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP). We help private and commercial operators navigate permits, ramp clearances, and compliance checks efficiently.

Our Services

We offer tailored CAAP Liaison services including:

  • Active coordination with CAAP for permits and approvals
  • Expedited ramp and airside access facilitation
  • Dedicated agents ensuring smooth passenger & cargo clearance
  • Private terminal and FBO coordination
  • Complete compliance and safety reporting
  • 24/7 on-demand CAAP operational support
